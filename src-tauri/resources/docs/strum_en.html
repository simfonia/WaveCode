🎸 Guitar Strumming

The Guitar Strumming system simulates the physical performance of a real guitar. It creates small time offsets based on the stroke direction.

Block Parameters

[Chord]: Select the chord to play (e.g., C, G, Am).
[Pattern]: Input the 16-beat rhythm notation.
[Instrument]: Select the instrument to trigger.

Notation Table (DSL)

CodeNameDescription
DFull DownstrokeSweeps from low strings to high strings.
UFull UpstrokeSweeps from high strings to low strings.
dHigh DownstrokeStrums only the treble strings (1-4).
uHigh UpstrokeStrums treble strings upward.
bBass NotePlays only the thickest bass string.
-SustainKeeps the strings ringing.
.RestNo action.

💡 Guitar Patch Setup Tips

For a believable guitar tone, ensure your Attack Time is extremely short. Here are recommended settings:

1. Folk Acoustic Style (Recommended)

OscillatorTriangle
ADSR (A, D, S, R)0.005, 0.8, 0.1, 0.6
Filter Freq / Q2500 Hz / 1.2
Reverb (Master Bus)Mix: 0.2 (Adds body and space)

2. Rock Overdrive Style

OscillatorSawtooth
ADSR (A, D, S, R)0.01, 0.5, 0.4, 0.3
Distortion Drive40 ~ 70
Filter Freq3500 Hz (Filters sharp harshness)
Pro Tip:
Add a Compressor to your Master Bus (Threshold: -24, Ratio: 4) to normalize volume spikes during strums for a professional polished sound.
